About

I'm Paul Powell from Maryland. I make music with AI because I love the sounds it helps me bring into the world: songs that encourage, uplift, and motivate people to feel good about themselves and make positive changes.

The songs lean toward rock but cover a lot of ground, from motivational anthems to tender love songs to just-for-fun tracks. New songs go up on YouTube regularly, and releases land on Spotify.

I feel like AI music is a great starting point and would love to see human artists take these AI creations to the next level.
