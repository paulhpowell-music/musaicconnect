Stories Behind the Songs

A little about the songs I make, one at a time.

From the Romantic Moonlight EP

Starlight in Your Eyes

This song is about how someone you love can make everything else go quiet. The picture I started with was a glow: "a glow in the night where your beauty resides," and starlight in someone's eyes pulling me in.

I kept the verses plain on purpose. "No makeup, no mask, just the truth of your grace" is the heart of it for me. It isn't about looking perfect, it's about being seen as you are, and next to that, "every light in this city fades." From there the song settles into "the hush of the evening, with the world moving slow," and I wanted the music to move at that same pace.

Some of my favorite lines are the small ones. A laugh that's "a melody carried on the breeze." A touch that's "a river putting my heart at ease." Then in the bridge the song admits it can't say it all: "No canvas could hold you, no poet could find the words for the way that your heart answers mine." So it ends with the simplest thing there is: "I'm yours, my sweet darling, forever and now."

I made it a slow, late-night R&B love song, the kind you put on low in the evening. Like all my songs, it was made with AI, from my ideas and direction. It's on my Romantic Moonlight EP, out October 30.
