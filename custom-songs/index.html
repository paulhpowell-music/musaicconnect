Gift ideas for a custom song

A song written just for someone is the kind of gift they'll play again and again. Here are a few moments where it fits best.

  • Anniversaries. I'll turn your story, from how you met to the little things only the two of you know, into a love song.
  • Birthdays. Instead of another card, give a song with their name, their quirks, and what they mean to you.
  • Weddings. A first-dance song or a surprise for the couple that no one else will have.
  • Encouragement. For a friend or family member facing a hard stretch, an uplifting song that says "I believe in you."
  • Tributes. A way to honor a parent, grandparent, or someone who has made a difference in your life.

Tell me a few details, like names, memories, and the mood you want, and I'll turn them into a song you can share with them.
