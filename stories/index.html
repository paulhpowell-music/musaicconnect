Stories Behind the Songs

A little about the songs I make, one at a time.

From the Romantic Moonlight EP

Starlight in Your Eyes

This song is for my wife. There's a mystery in looking into her eyes that I never get tired of, and "soft starlight in your eyes keeps on pulling me inside" is how it felt when I tried to put it into words.

I really appreciate the quiet times we have alone together. The song is about just being ourselves, enjoying that time, and appreciating each other for who we really are. That's where "no makeup, no mask, just the truth of your grace" comes from, and the "hush of the evening, with the world moving slow."

The bridge says it better than I can: "No canvas could hold you, no poet could find the words for the way that your heart answers mine." So it ends with the simplest thing I know: "I'm yours, my sweet darling, forever and now."

It's a slow R&B love song. Like all my songs, it was made with AI, from my ideas and direction. It's on my Romantic Moonlight EP, out October 30.
